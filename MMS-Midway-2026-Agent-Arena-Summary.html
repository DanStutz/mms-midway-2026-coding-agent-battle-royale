An evidence-led look at reliability, agent time and estimated token value. A fast finish gets attention. A working result earns trust.

Read this as an execution report, not a code-quality leaderboard. “Finished app” means the harness completed and produced changed HTML. Custom demos have no automated functional or visual grade. The nine graded rehearsal attempts all passed and are excluded from the default live-task ranking.
Stopped races stay in the ledger and are excluded from rankings. Failed attempts stay in the denominator.
